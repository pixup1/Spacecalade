# Spacecalade

In Spacecalade, you must escape a destroyed space station after a mysterious catastrophe took place while you were sleeping. Marooned in the middle of the Milky Way, 3.2 lightyears from the closest star, your only chance of survival is the last remaining escape pod, at the other end of the wreck. One wrong jump and you may end up drifting away from the station until your oxygen runs out. Will you make it out?

## Game design considerations

**Elevator pitch:** Spacecalade is a 2D platforming game about escaping a space station by going up a series of levels that each introduce a new level design element. The main mechanic of the game is to jump between walls to go up, while avoiding to lose control by bouncing off them or throwing yourself in the void.

**Target audience:** This game is aimed at the people who participate in the game jam. I feel like a casual platformer with an interesting movement mechanic is a safe bet for most people, and it is also a safe bet as a developer because I have never participated in a game jam or even made a proper game before.

**Mechanics:** Leaping from wall to wall in zero gravity is an interesting base mechanic. It is coupled with a lack of air friction, which elevates the sense of risk because you can only stop moving if you hit a wall at the right angle.

I have thought of a few different level design mechanics, such as moving platforms, physics-enabled platforms which react to you jumping on them, enemies on a path, homing missiles, platforms you cannot grab and must bounce off, a jetpack that gives you a "double jump", a little black hole that produces gravity, etc. I think most of these will be introduced in their own levels. The first one will simply get you used to the movement mechanics, with the main threat being the void.

Does the game need more core mechanics? Like a limited oxygen supply that you must remember to refill? idk

**Dynamics:** Part of the feeling I am trying to convey is similar to the one you can feel the movie Gravity (2013), when Sandra Bullock is untethered outside the ISS, trying desperately to grab anything she can, and messing up because of the panic. Seeing a similar feeling on a player would be great.

In a broader sense, I like these kinds of game where any small mistake can make you go back to the beginning of a level.

**Why would I want to play this game:** Like most semi-challenging platformers, it is about proving to yourself that you have still got it, and are worthy of calling yourself a gamer.

I want this game to be pretty challenging, but getting it to be challenging (without being unfair or requiring pixel-perfect jumps) is going to be challenging, because the mechanics give a lot of freedom to the player. I must find ways to counter-balance that.

## Levels

### Level 1

Intro cinematic

Teaches leaping, grabbing, dying in the void, dying to a deadly wall, and bouncing.

**Specific mechanics:**
- Walls that you can only bounce off (can't grab them)
- Walls that kill you

**Parts of the level:**
+ Simple leaping tutorial, from one wall to another
+ Introduction of the void
+ Introduction of slippy walls with a trap
+ Use of a slippy wall to get around a threat
+ To end the level, camera pulls back, big leap to another part of the station
